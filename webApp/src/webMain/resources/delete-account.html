Delete your Muster account

You can delete your account yourself, in the app or on the web.

How to delete it

  1. Open the Muster app, or @WEB_APP_URL@, and sign in with your email.
  2. Tap Settings at the top of the home screen.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your group memberships, event RSVPs and standby places.
  • Group invitations sent to your address.

What stays

  • Groups and events you created stay for their other members, no longer linked to you.
  • If you are the only admin of a group, that group is archived and hidden from all its members. The app lists these groups before you confirm.

Deletion happens straight away and can't be undone.

Can't sign in? Email @CONTACT_EMAIL@ from the email address you use for Muster, and we'll delete your account for you.
